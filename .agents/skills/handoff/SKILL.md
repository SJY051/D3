# Handoff

Read the issue, requirement, worktree, and latest verification output. Produce one handoff containing:

- issue and requirement IDs;
- intended outcome and current state;
- changed and relevant untouched files;
- decisions and service boundaries that must survive;
- exact checks with pass, fail, skip, and not-run;
- blockers, risks, and the smallest next step.

Do not commit, push, merge, deploy, or update an external tracker. Finish when a fresh teammate can resume without reconstructing hidden context.
